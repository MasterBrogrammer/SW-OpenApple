OpenApple extra disks — licenses and sources
============================================

Only titles with a public-domain, freeware, historic-system, or OSI-style
grant are bundled. Commercial Apple II software (Choplifter, Karateka,
Lode Runner, Oregon Trail, Atarisoft arcade ports, …) is not included.
Drop your own .dsk / .woz in the app.

DOS 3.3 System Master (from apple2js JSON)
  Historic Apple system software. Real catalog (this image has no Apple Trek):
  A  LITTLE BRICK OUT, COLOR DEMOSOFT, BRIAN'S THEME
  I  ANIMALS, APPLEVISION, BIORHYTHM  (need DOS command INT first)
  Autostart HELLO, then we wait for ] or > and RUN.
